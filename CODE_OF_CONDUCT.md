# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a respectful and harassment-free experience for
everyone, whatever their background, experience, identity or opinions.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good faith.
- Disagree with ideas and code, not with people.
- Give feedback that is specific and kind, and accept it the same way.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or an email, without
  their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to spaces run for this project, including its issues, pull requests,
discussions and review comments, and to someone representing the project in public.

## Reporting

To report a conduct problem, write to **help@baragaun.com**. Please include what happened,
where, and who was involved. Do not use public issues for conduct reports, and do not use
the [security reporting route](SECURITY.md); that is for vulnerabilities.

The maintainers will read reports and decide what to do. Actions can range from a private
word to removing comments or blocking someone from the project. We will try to treat a
reporter's identity with discretion, but we cannot promise outcomes or timelines.
